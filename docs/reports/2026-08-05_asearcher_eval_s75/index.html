ASearcher Evaluation — 8B step-75 checkpoints

2026-08-05 · Qwen3-8B-Base, 32-turn wiki-18 local RAG · 7,152 questions over 10 benchmarks · greedy decoding · scored by strict EM, sub-EM, and gpt-oss-120b judge (frozen prompt asearcher_mbe_v1)

1 Key findings

  • turn-PPO transfers ahead of GRPO, consistent with training val but with a much smaller margin: wiki-answerable macro judge 0.550 vs 0.535 (strict EM 0.405 vs 0.395, sub-EM 0.461 vs 0.446). Training val said 0.561 vs 0.520.
  • turn-PPO reaches that score with 2–4× fewer turns (4.3–12.3 turns/question vs GRPO's uniform 16.5–18.1) — same answer quality at a fraction of the search budget.
  • Strict EM (the training reward) understates real accuracy by ~0.13–0.14: of 7,152 judged trajectories per arm, ~930–974 are judge-correct but EM-wrong, while only 3 are the reverse.
  • The live-web trio (GAIA / frames / xbench) floors at 0.05–0.23 judge — a corpus gap (our retriever serves wiki-18 only), not a method difference.
  • GRPO leaves 8.0% of questions with no <answer> at all (loops to the turn cap); turn-PPO only 0.5%.

4 Search efficiency

GRPO runs essentially every question to near the turn cap (16.5–18.1 avg turns, uniformly — including single-hop questions answerable in one search). turn-PPO modulates its budget with difficulty: ~4–5 turns on single-hop, 8–12 on multi-hop. At equal-or-better accuracy this is a 2–4× cheaper policy at inference time, and matches the training-side observation (turn-PPO 6.5 searches/question vs GRPO 16.1 at step 75).

6 Method & provenance

  • Checkpoints: token_grpo_qwen3-8b-base_32turn_fast75_s0 and turn_ppo_b0_qwen3-8b-base_32turn_fast75_s0 at step 75 (75 steps, unfiltered Base-35k, fast config: partial rollout 8/4 + dynamic token batching + chunked prefill).
  • Eval protocol: val_only greedy rollouts, 32-turn cap, top-5 wiki-18 e5 retrieval, identical env to training; full trajectory dumps on HDFS (logs/p8b_eval_{grpo,turnppo}_s75/rollout_eval_*.jsonl.zst).
  • Scoring: scripts/asearcher/judge_rollouts.py — dump → trajectory fold → gold join → concurrent judging (gpt-oss-120b, TP4, mxfp4) → per-benchmark aggregation. Summaries in outputs/judge/*.summary.json; cross-arm table outputs/judge/cross_arm_s75.md.
  • Training context: val curves (strict EM, greedy, 512 fixed questions): GRPO 0.195 → 0.227 → 0.520 → 0.520; turn-PPO 0.195 → 0.234 → 0.479 → 0.561 @ steps 0/25/50/75.

7 Caveats & pending

Read before quoting.
  • Single seed. One run per arm; the wiki-7 gap (judge +0.015) is small — seed replication is queued before any headline claim.
  • Unfiltered training data. Both arms trained on Base-35k without the closed-book filter (~40% of unfiltered training successes need zero searches) — the absolute numbers carry a recall confound; the 4B filtered campaign inverted the arm ordering.
  • Live-web ≠ method comparison. GAIA/frames/xbench need the open web; our retriever serves wiki-18 (2018 snapshot). Those columns measure the corpus gap.
  • An earlier (2026-08-04 morning) claim that transfer "reverses" training val was wrong — based on a mis-computed turn-PPO macro (0.355). The judged full-dump numbers here supersede it.
  • Pending: the three in-flight arms (hcapo_ans, token_ppo, gigpo) get the same eval + judge pass when they reach step 75; this report then extends to the full 5-arm estimator comparison.

\section*{TL;DR (preliminary)}
The fast config is \textbf{healthy, OOM-free at the full 24{,}576 token budget}, and cuts
update-side phases $\sim$\textbf{2.5--4$\times$}; generation is unchanged ($\pm$7\%, within the
KV-fraction confound). Matched first-two-cycles wall-clock: \textbf{3{,}053\,s vs 3{,}848\,s}
(1.26$\times$; identical released counts 325/700). Projected 150-step GRPO arm:
\textbf{$\sim$6.5\,d} (from 8.9\,d partial / 13.3\,d vanilla). All speedup is attributable to
\textbf{dynamic micro-batching}; chunked prefill contributed $\approx$0 at these prompt lengths.
One \flag{prominent flag} (\S4): the \code{actor/entropy\_loss} \emph{metric} reads
$\sim$2$\times$ lower under packing --- a measurement artifact, not a policy change (evidence
inside); all behavioral metrics match baseline within noise.

\section*{3. Attribution}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Dynamic micro-batching (DYNBSZ)}: actor update 2.8$\times$, old\_log\_prob
2.5$\times$, ref 2.5$\times$ at matched cycle 2 (967$\to$342, 195$\to$79, 195$\to$78\,s).
This is the entire measured gain.
\item \textbf{Chunked prefill}: gen 1753$\to$1874\,s at cycle 2 (\emph{$+$7\%}) --- no benefit at
our prompt lengths, and the sign is within the KV 0.6$\to$0.5 confound. Keep (harmless) but
attribute nothing to it.
\item Generation now dominates: $\sim$80\% of fast step time $\Rightarrow$ \textbf{active-slot
packing} (skip finished trajectories in gen) is the next real lever.
\end{itemize}

\section*{4. Sanity: training metrics vs baseline \hfill \flag{one metric flagged}}
Matched pre-update step 1: success 0.218 vs 0.237, response length 430 vs $\sim$500 tok,
episode turns and partial counters in band; step-2 success 0.203 vs 0.184. All within
temp-1.0 sampling noise. \flag{Flag:} \code{actor/entropy\_loss} reads 1.05/1.22 (steps 1/2)
vs baseline 2.07/2.38 --- $\sim$2$\times$ lower \emph{at identical initial weights on step 1},
while the rollouts themselves are statistically indistinguishable. Since the entropy metric is
computed during the old\_log\_prob recompute and aggregated over micro-batches, token-budget
packing changes the \emph{weighting of the average}, not the per-token log-probs; entropy has
\code{coeff=0} and never enters the loss. Classification: \textbf{measurement artifact
(high confidence)} --- to be re-verified in the final report by recomputing token-weighted
entropy from identical rollout dumps; if it were a true policy-distribution change it would
have to appear in step-1 rollouts, and it does not.
